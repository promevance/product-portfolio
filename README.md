# Product Portfolio

Product marketing and growth work on real startup products, by **Evan Promise Chukwubueze**: brand and growth specialist and GTM engineer.

Each folder documents one product engagement: the product and its market, my role, how growth was driven, the tools and systems used, the results, and supporting material such as briefs, reports and campaign assets. The focus is on **how growth happened and what it achieved**, not on design or code.

## Products

| Folder | Product | My role | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`kompletecare/`](kompletecare/) | **KompleteCare**: a health-tech marketplace connecting hospitals, HMOs, laboratories, pharmacies, doctors and patients | Product Marketing & Growth (B2B focus) | B2B lead generation, onboarding and pilot-to-paid conversion; 600% growth in B2B adoption and a more than 300% increase in overall platform usage |
| [`beacons-tetradinc/`](beacons-tetradinc/) | **SimlessPay and TetradPay**: two fintech remittance platforms, worked on through Beacons Agency | Team Lead, Marketing and Growth | Lifecycle, CRM and community-led growth; user base grown from 50K to 90K+ in four months without sustained paid ads |

Open a folder to read the full write-up in its README.
